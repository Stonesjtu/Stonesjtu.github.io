What I work on

From Models to Production

I work across model serving, training systems, and hardware optimization to make AI practical, efficient, and accessible.

  • Model serving: Deploy and optimize LLMs, VLMs, and GUI agents for Doubao mobile assistant, focusing on latency, throughput, and cost.
  • Training systems: Integrate distributed training stacks, including VeOmni and verl on Ascend 910B.
  • Hardware optimization: Improve model execution through AI compilers, NPU operators, and GPU kernels.
