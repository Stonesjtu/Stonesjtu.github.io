## What is watermark?

Identify whether a passage was generated by my model.

## Why do we use watermark?

- Regulation: EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content
- Anti-LLMs: paper, homework, others

## How to add watermark?


### Naive way

```
It's a good day!

Authored by: CLAUDE
```



```
It's |CLAUDE| a |CLAUDE| good |CLAUDE| day |CLAUDE|!
```

## Challenges in Inference Systems

1. Gumbel-max sampling overhead (negligible)
2. Speculative Decode (Rejection sampling versus Gumbel-max sampling)

## FAQs

Does watermarking affect LLM's output?

No in theory.

No in Google's 20 million Gemini thumbs-up and thumbs-down A/B tests.[^synthid-paper]

Anthropic reports no observed impact on content, creativity, or readability in its internal testing.[^anthropic]
